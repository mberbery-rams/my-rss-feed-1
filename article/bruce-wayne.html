Bruce Wayne Returns as Chair of Wayne Enterprises

Gotham Business Daily · Corporate desk

Bruce Wayne has been appointed chair of Wayne Enterprises with immediate effect, the company confirmed on Thursday, returning to the board eleven years after stepping back from an executive role. The appointment follows the departure of the previous chair, who left in September after a disagreement with directors over the pace of the group's restructuring programme.

In a short statement issued from the company's Gotham City headquarters, Wayne said his immediate priority was the applied sciences division and a review of the group's real estate holdings, which he described as "carrying more balance sheet than they earn". He gave no timetable for the review and did not confirm whether disposals were contemplated.

Analysts covering the group described the appointment as stabilising but noted it concentrates considerable influence in a single figure at a point when the board is already under pressure over succession planning. Two of the four analysts polled after the announcement moved their recommendation to hold from buy, citing governance rather than trading concerns.

Wayne Enterprises employs roughly 34,000 people worldwide, with about 9,000 based in Gotham City. The group reported flat revenue and a narrowing operating margin in its most recent half-year results, and has signalled it will update the market on the restructuring at its capital markets day in the first quarter.
