Wayne Enterprises Opens Metropolis Battery Plant

Metropolis Industrial Wire · Manufacturing desk

Wayne Enterprises has opened a battery cell facility on the eastern edge of Metropolis, the group's first manufacturing investment in the municipality. The plant will employ approximately 700 staff at full capacity and is expected to reach volume production in the second half of next year following a phased commissioning programme.

The facility covers 480,000 square feet on a site the group acquired four years ago. Output is contracted almost entirely to two industrial customers under long-term supply agreements, a structure the division's president said was intended to remove volume risk from the ramp-up period rather than to maximise margin in the early years.

Local officials welcomed the opening, pointing to the municipality's efforts to attract advanced manufacturing after two decades of decline in its heavy industrial base. A spokesperson confirmed that the project received an abatement on municipal property taxes over a twelve-year term, tied to headcount commitments that are reviewed annually.

The division has indicated that a second phase, roughly doubling installed capacity, remains under evaluation and would depend on demand signals from existing offtake partners. No decision is expected before the end of the next financial year. Construction of the first phase ran approximately five months behind the original schedule, which the group attributed to delays in the delivery of coating equipment.
