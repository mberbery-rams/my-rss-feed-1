Wayne Manor Sells for $47m in Gotham's Largest Estate Deal

Gotham Property Review · Staff reporter

Wayne Manor, the 42-acre estate on the northern ridge of Gotham City, has changed hands for $47 million in what local agents are calling the largest single-asset residential transaction the city has recorded in a decade. The sale closed late on Tuesday after a competitive process that drew four bidders, two of them from outside the region.

The buyer, a private investment vehicle that has not been named, intends to retain the main house and convert the east wing and stable block into a small hospitality operation. Planning officers confirmed that a pre-application enquiry covering roughly 18,000 square feet of the estate was lodged with the city in the spring, though no formal consent has yet been granted.

Agents active in the Gotham market said the price reflects both the scarcity of large single-title estates inside the city boundary and a broader recovery in trophy assets after two slow years. "There is no comparable lot within thirty miles," one adviser said. "When something like Wayne Manor trades, it resets the ceiling for everything below it."

The estate's gross internal area is understood to exceed 38,000 square feet across the principal residence and outbuildings. Local records show the property had been held by the same owner for several decades and had not been openly marketed before this year, which advisers said contributed to the level of interest once it reached the market.

Completion is expected within sixty days. The vendor's advisers declined to comment on the allocation of proceeds or on whether adjoining parcels might follow to market.
