Harbourline wraps Fund II fundraising at $1.75bn, doubling its predecessor

Private credit firm Harbourline Capital has wrapped up fundraising for Harbourline Senior Lending Fund II, hitting a $1.75bn hard cap against a $1.2bn target. The vehicle targets senior secured, mid-market direct lending and attracted insurers, pension plans and a sovereign wealth fund.

Managing Partner Tomás Reyes said demand reflects private credit's shift into investors' core allocations rather than a niche exposure. Fund II is about twice the size of Harbourline Capital's previous fund and is already around 20% invested.

Harbourline Capital plans to concentrate the fund on defensive, non-cyclical borrowers, extending floating-rate senior loans as it scales its lending platform.
