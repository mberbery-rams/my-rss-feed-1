Harbourline Capital closes Senior Lending Fund II at $1.75bn hard cap

Harbourline Capital has held the final close of Harbourline Senior Lending Fund II at its $1.75 billion hard cap, exceeding an initial $1.2 billion target. The fund will make senior secured loans to mid-market companies and drew commitments from pension plans, insurers and a sovereign wealth fund.

"Investors are allocating to private credit as a core holding, not a satellite," said Tomás Reyes, Managing Partner at Harbourline Capital. Fund II is roughly double the size of its predecessor and has already deployed about 20% of its capital.

Harbourline Capital said the vehicle would focus on defensive, non-cyclical sectors and floating-rate senior debt as it builds out its direct lending platform.
