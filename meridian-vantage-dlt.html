Vantage Logistics taps Meridian Credit Partners for $450m private credit loan

Vantage Logistics has secured a $450m unitranche loan from private credit manager Meridian Credit Partners, sidestepping the broadly syndicated loan market as it pushes further into Sun Belt warehousing. The senior secured facility funds the company's purchase of three distribution centers and refinances existing bank borrowings.

Elena Marsh, Meridian Credit Partners' Managing Partner, said the transaction reflects continued borrower appetite for flexible, privately negotiated credit. The floating-rate financing was arranged bilaterally rather than through a bank syndicate.

The loan ranks among the quarter's larger direct lending deals in the industrials and logistics space. Meridian Credit Partners has been among the most active mid-market private credit lenders this year.
