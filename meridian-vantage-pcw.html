Meridian Credit Partners provides $450m unitranche facility to Vantage Logistics

Meridian Credit Partners has provided a $450 million senior secured unitranche facility to Vantage Logistics, backing the mid-market logistics operator's expansion across the US Sun Belt. The direct lending package, one of the largest in the sector this quarter, will finance Vantage Logistics' acquisition of three regional distribution centers and refinance existing bank debt.

"Vantage is exactly the kind of resilient, cash-generative business our direct lending strategy is built around," said Elena Marsh, Managing Partner at Meridian Credit Partners. The all-private-credit financing replaces a broadly syndicated bank loan and carries a floating rate.

Meridian Credit Partners has now deployed more than $6 billion across the logistics and industrials sectors, and said borrower demand for flexible, privately negotiated credit remains strong heading into the second half of the year.
